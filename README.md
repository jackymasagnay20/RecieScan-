# ReceiScan – Smart Receipt Scanning & Expense Management System

*Transforming paper receipts into organized digital financial records.*

## Project Overview

ReceiScan is an innovative, web-based receipt scanning and expense management system designed to simplify how individuals and businesses record, organize, and monitor their financial transactions.

Powered by *Optical Character Recognition (OCR)* technology, ReceiScan aims to automate the extraction of essential information from physical and digital receipts, including merchant names, transaction dates, purchased items, and total amounts.

By combining intelligent data processing with an intuitive interface, the system seeks to reduce manual data entry, minimize recordkeeping errors, and provide a centralized platform for managing receipts and tracking expenses.

## Key Features

- *Smart Receipt Scanning:* Capture receipts using a device camera or upload existing receipt images.
- *OCR-Powered Data Extraction:* Automatically recognize and extract relevant transaction details from receipt images.
- *Digital Receipt Management:* Securely store, organize, retrieve, update, and delete receipt records.
- *Expense Tracking:* Monitor transactions and maintain an organized history of expenses.
- *Automatic Expense Categorization:* Classify purchases into categories such as food, transportation, shopping, and utilities.
- *Advanced Search and Filtering:* Locate receipts using merchant names, transaction dates, categories, or amounts.
- *Expense Analytics and Reports:* Visualize spending patterns through summaries, charts, and reports.
- *User-Friendly Dashboard:* Access receipts, expense records, and financial insights through a centralized interface.

## How ReceiScan Works

1. *Receipt Upload:* Users capture or upload a receipt image through the web application.
2. *Image Processing:* The system prepares the uploaded image for text recognition.
3. *OCR Extraction:* Tesseract OCR identifies and extracts text from the receipt.
4. *Data Validation:* Extracted information is processed and presented for user verification or correction.
5. *Secure Storage:* Confirmed receipt details are saved to the PostgreSQL database.
6. *Expense Organization:* Transactions are categorized and organized for convenient tracking.
7. *Reporting and Analytics:* Users can access their expense history, summaries, and spending insights.
